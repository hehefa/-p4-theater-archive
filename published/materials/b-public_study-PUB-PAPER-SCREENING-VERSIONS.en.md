# Paper Screening: versions of participation and circulation

Thirty public studies. Anonymous method analyses and credited documents state their evidence separately. Interpretation does not establish implementation; this is not the full research collection.

English names and titles are editorial working translations. Publication dates, planned dates and actual event dates remain distinct; a published recollection is not independent verification.

## What the public material records

The 20 November 2020 article describes participants choosing materials, making images and exchanging with others, crediting Lisa / Xue Lixia with developing and facilitating the format with P4. The 26 November version links making, exploration with a partner, sharing and documentation into a stated procedure. Recruitment conditions also differ: early-registration and group-member discounts in the earlier text become a sharing-related friendship price in the later one. The editorial interpretation is that participation and circulation are explicitly linked in that version. The texts do not establish the reason for the change, actual payments or therapeutic effects; photographs and repeated feedback cannot reconstruct a continuous event.

## Time and version

**Original publication**：2020-11-20 / 2020-11-26

Comparison of public recruitment and procedure versions, not an audit of payments or therapeutic outcomes.

Editorial research from the cited published or retained documents; interpretations are not quotations from participants.

- A registration contact is not thereby the author of artworks, photographs or every article.

These are reviewed research texts. Related public films identify their access editions separately; local source timings are not converted into web links.
