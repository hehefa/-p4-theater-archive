4月8日文章把招募段放在余裕雨木五节提案之前，并在招募段明确八名创作者、专业与年龄不限。4月6日导言已提出八人，不能把人数视为此次首次新增；两篇可见的五节提案正文一致，记录的是邀请编排的变化。

The 8 April article places recruitment before Yu Yu Yumu’s five-part proposal and explicitly invites eight creators of any age or professional background. Eight already appears in the 6 April introduction, and the visible five-part proposal is identical across both pages. This documents a change in how the invitation is arranged.
