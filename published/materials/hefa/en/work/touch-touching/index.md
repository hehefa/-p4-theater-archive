## From photographs to a projectable image

This 2014 work appears alongside the Wu images in the same portfolio, but its medium is specified as “any material suitable for projection,” with dimensions determined by the exhibition. That specification allows the image to leave a fixed print format and enter another surface and scale.

## Contact within the image

Green and blue-black gaps form channels between rounded orange-red clusters. The eye moves between coloured masses and their edges; contact can first be read as shapes approaching, crowding and separating. This tactile association arises from the visible image. The portfolio’s title and medium alone do not establish touch sensors or audience interaction. The source page also specifies neither a separate running time nor a particular installation.

Archive reading, 30 September 2026 · Sources: He Fa Portfolio (PDF), p. 21.
