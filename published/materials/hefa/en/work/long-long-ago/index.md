## Working together in the proposal

The proposal identifies shared experience, life, time and space as its starting points. It envisages selecting artists from different districts of San Francisco to form a new collective, then travelling between their districts to hold events. Each participating artist would host and curate the event in their own area. Performance, installation, exhibition and other forms were encouraged, in homes or public spaces.

The text sets out recruitment, announcements and a year-long sequence of events, through which different communities might encounter one another repeatedly. These are plans within a proposal, not evidence of a realized exhibition or public programme. The ideas and timetable are retained as a document of possible collective work. The body names the group; He Fa’s specific responsibilities require further documentation.

## Archive note

Based on Fa He_AltEx_LongLongAgo.docx. The dates 2017–2019 refer to the application milestones and planned events in the proposal, not a verified production or realization period. The proposed 27 districts, 54-week schedule and participant numbers are not reported as completed outcomes. The original English project name is retained.
