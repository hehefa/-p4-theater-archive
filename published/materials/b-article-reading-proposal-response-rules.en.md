# Proposal, response and rules

Speaking and deciding within Purple Action

Find the eight-plus-eight proposal, check what the response promises, then open the rule pages. In the first card, distinguish numbers, speaking time and possible voting positions after leaving the candidate group. In the second, compare the promised investigation with the principle of discussion that is retained. In the third, examine rules written as provisions and ballots. The cards organize a comparison of documents, not a timeline of completed reforms. The forum and organizations are fictional settings within the work.

## 2 What does the response promise?

It helps distinguish an institutional response and a promise to investigate from an explicit decision to adopt a proposal.

The promise to investigate and adjust, the scope of discussion and the artwork notice

[Read: The planning group’s response ↗](https://p4theater.cn/articles/2021/2021-08-24-紫色行动-最终解决方案官方严正回应舆论吐槽-39031c6a5d/)

**Documented details**

The fictional planning group promises to investigate problems and adjust the parameters of the Kuijian Council, while opposing an advance restriction of discussion to a particular theoretical form. It does not explicitly adopt eight-plus-eight seats, five-minute statements or voting rights for those leaving the candidate group.

## 3 How rules become a page

It gives procedure a visible form through provisions, rounds, spaces for reasons and pages about circulation.

Page 7: rule design; page 8: ballot collage; page 9: circulation; page 10: deliberation and voting methods

[Read: Purple Action: the 24-page project introduction](https://b.p4theater.cn/assets/cross-volume-materials/cv-purple-action-dca427eb9eaa-dca427eb9eaa.pdf#page=7)

**Documented details**

Page 7 of the public 24-page PDF describes rule design and institutions opened to participants. The ballot collage on page 8 shows rounds and spaces for reasons. Pages 9–10 describe circulation, feedback, deliberation and voting models. Read these pages for how procedures are written, recorded and passed on.

## Look closely

- How is the amount of speaking time connected to the position from which someone decides?

- Which concerns does the response take up, and which position does it retain?

- What judgments become recordable when a decision is written on a ballot with a round and a space for reasons?

The forum, organizations and role signatures are fictional settings within the artwork. They do not identify real-person disputes or actual harm. Adoption of the proposal is not established, and the ballot collage is not a complete record of any one vote.

When a rule allows speech, does it also shape how speech can be heard?
