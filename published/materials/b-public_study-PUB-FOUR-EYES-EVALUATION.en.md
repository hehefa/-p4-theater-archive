## Research narrative: evidence limits below

The retained post-performance research notes suggest three distinct questions: how character relationships are understood, how a performance style is received, and how interaction affects viewing. These criteria need not produce the same answer. A researcher can record them separately and ask which staging choices relate to each response; liking a passage is not a proxy for understanding the whole work. Only this anonymous analytical method is presented here, without judgments about particular participants or profiles of anyone’s attitudes or abilities.
