# Howlers: an edited chorus of production roles

Thirty public studies. Anonymous method analyses and credited documents state their evidence separately. Interpretation does not establish implementation; this is not the full research collection.

English names and titles are editorial working translations. Publication dates, planned dates and actual event dates remain distinct; a published recollection is not independent verification.

## What the public material records

The article published on 31 December 2020 places brief statements by writers, director, sound and stage designers, performers, 3P, photographers and publicity workers side by side, followed by an announcement for 2 January 2021 at 19:30. Rumeng is credited as initiator and writer, Jinzi as director, Dalin for sound design and He Fa as a 3P member. Stage designer Luo Chunping mentions stage limitations, but the text does not identify the constraint or its resolution. The edited statements document public perspectives across roles, not the sequence of a live conversation or evidence of interpersonal disagreement.
