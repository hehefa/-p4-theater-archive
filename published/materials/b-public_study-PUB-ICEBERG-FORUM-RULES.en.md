# After Iceberg: from offering a view to entering a role

Thirty public studies. Anonymous method analyses and credited documents state their evidence separately. Interpretation does not establish implementation; this is not the full research collection.

English names and titles are editorial working translations. Publication dates, planned dates and actual event dates remain distinct; a published recollection is not independent verification.

## What the public material records

The Beifeng forum recruitment published on 12 January 2022 specifies opportunities to play different roles, comment on situations and directing choices, and intervene while respecting a character’s values and attitudes. It announces an activity for 19 January at 19:00. The methodological question is how a view becomes a playable proposal that others can answer. This is a documented mechanism for moving from commentary toward role-based practice, not evidence that the activity answered one particular spectator, occurred as scheduled or resolved earlier production problems.
