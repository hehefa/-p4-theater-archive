## A reason for education does not end the questioning

[Approximate start 01:10 · education funding ↗](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=32m22TB5dTw&t=70s)

If someone wants money to improve education, does that make the desire less greedy? Early in Happiness Kingdom, Unit E, that justification meets further questions. A respondent explains what the money would be used for; the discussion returns to wanting more. Can a proposed use change the judgement of a desire? ## From an educational path to running a noodle shop

[Approximate start 07:28 · the noodle-shop proposal ↗](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=32m22TB5dTw&t=448s)

Later, another choice is proposed: opening a beef-noodle shop. The discussion turns to beef, making broth, whether the business can make money, and operating costs. A judgement about education becomes a livelihood proposal that can be questioned detail by detail. Once another path is suggested, how would it actually work?[[V1]](https://b.p4theater.cn/en/research/happiness-kingdom-education-and-noodles#ref-v1)

The noodle-shop turn makes the exchange concrete. The respondent must address practical details as well as state a choice. The turn leaves a practical question: how does a proposed choice become an arrangement whose details can be discussed?

Published on 19 July, the recruitment notice lists a person's capacities to breathe, sense and move, then makes continued dissatisfaction part of its theatrical accusation.
