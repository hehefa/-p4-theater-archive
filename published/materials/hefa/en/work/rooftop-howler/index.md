## Project statement

Howlers on the Rooftop was developed through P4U, leaving open calls, rehearsal materials, participant texts, a public performance and later conversations. The performance took place at P4 at 19:30 on 2 January 2021.

Alongside his organisational work through P4, He Fa joined Zhang Shaohua and Huang Lucang in the 3P group’s live documentation.

## The Rooftop Howler

Does participating in a work mean agreeing with it?

### Begin with what happens in the work

The archive contains recruitment, rehearsal, character texts, performance and post-performance interviews.

In a published interview, Qingyi says the unfamiliar open format strengthened his preference for conventional theatre; Reinate expresses dissatisfaction with the script and room for revision.

A character who keeps howling makes the fictional city ask what his voice means. Discussion around the production returns that question to its making: who can speak, whose views alter the structure, and who stays chiefly to complete the performance? The published interviews are valuable because they do not polish every experience into the same story of growth. Some value the company more than the script; others leave more certain that they want a different kind of theatre.

These disagreements are not decorative signs of authenticity. They test what openness actually enabled. Giving them a central place allows source material to resist the portfolio’s positive account. Yet one participant’s allegation about another person should not become an adjudicated fact. The accurate form retains who said what, when, and leaves room for further responses. Documentation gains value here by letting a work remain open to judgment after its performance ends.

### The limits of this reading

Criticism in the interview is an attributed account, not a factual ruling by this site. Characters are not equated with their performers.

Based on performance records and the first Howler backstage interview, published 28 March 2021. Interviewees’ views retain their attribution.
