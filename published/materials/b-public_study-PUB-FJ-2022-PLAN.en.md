## What the public material records

The notice published on 31 March 2022 announced a participatory event initiated by Beifeng Theatre Group for the evening of 1 April at Jingshan Park. Its opening cancellation claim is immediately reversed. The synopsis describes performers who have not reached mutual understanding; this fictional framing is not independent evidence of what happened in rehearsals.
