## 公开正文

9月2日文章从演完之后的反应谈起，将公演视作排演过程中的结果，并追问作品的批判锋芒。仅采用演后讨论与作品问题，不转述私人恋爱关系判断，也不据发表日期确定具体演出场次。

The 2 September article begins with responses after a performance, treats presentation as an outcome of rehearsal and questions the work’s critical edge. Only discussion of the performance and artistic question is used. Private relationship judgments are excluded, and publication date does not identify a specific performance.
