## The production and He Fa’s writing

This P4U production explores romantic desire, rational restraint and choices within relationships. Its public introduction gives contrasting positions to different characters and makes audience choices part of the story’s direction. Actors and audiences share the space, and watching includes interaction and decision. The contemporary credits identify the responsibilities of Chen Yu, Hu Junyuan, Zhang Shaohua and other collaborators.

On the same day, He Fa published “Who Are the Centrists?”, using repetition, abrupt transitions and wordplay to create another narrative strand. His centrists wander, hesitate and wait to act; the closing passage returns the demand for a decision to the reader. Read beside the production introduction, the text shows how P4’s public writing participates in bringing a work into being: it communicates the schedule while establishing unsettled positions and emotions.

## Role within this archive

This entry includes He Fa’s directly associated writing and preserves the production’s original credits. The poster, character dialogue and article together provide an entry into how the work was proposed within P4U’s framework of a single public performance.
