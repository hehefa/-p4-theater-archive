## 公开正文

独立 Statement 图像先声明房间中每一项都经过设计且有意义，随后以粗体安排阅读：不理解时先怀疑自己、再怀疑作者、再怀疑自己；仍不理解便重复，理解后才继续。结尾把钥匙、面前的门、开门与关门写成后续动作，并声称关门后不再徘徊，因为已经知道怎样寻找钥匙。这里描述可读文本的指令，不证明现场存在相应布置或观众实际照做。

The standalone Statement image declares every item in the room designed and meaningful. Bold instructions direct a reader who does not understand to doubt themselves, then the author, then themselves again; repeat if necessary and continue when understanding arrives. The ending moves through a key, a door, opening and closing it, and claims that the reader will no longer linger because they know how to find the key. These are readable instructions, not verified room arrangements or observed audience actions.
