## 公开正文

3月4日通知转述上周玩家时间不足的反馈，记述玩家箜发起《恋爱加强局》，并分别列梁吟为带领者、陈晨为特邀嘉宾。计划3月6日14:30–18:00开展，增加前半小时肢体联结热身；总时长增加的半小时也被新热身占用，不能证明原有练习延长、热身实施或时间问题解决。

The 4 March notice relayed feedback about insufficient time and described player Kong initiating an intensified Love Session. It separately credited Liang Yin as facilitator and Chen Chen as invited guest. The plan scheduled March 6, 14:30–18:00, with physical connection as a first-half-hour warm-up. The added half-hour overall was also occupied by the new warm-up; this does not establish longer existing exercises, delivery of the warm-up or resolution of the time shortage.
