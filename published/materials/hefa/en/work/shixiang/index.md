PHOTOGRAPHY THEATRE / BEIJING

## Make a portrait. Then face it together.

Shixiang is a P4 Theater photography-theatre project concerned with one’s own image. Practice began in 2019; a public account of its format followed in 2020. Zhang Shaohua and He Fa developed it together. Zhang worked on recruitment and the practical encounters; He Fa’s 2020 account describes his contribution to theory, structure, framework and interpretation.

The published introduction traces a movement from conversation in a white room to photography on a dark stage. A large-format camera and lighting become objects within that stage. Participants subsequently return to view and discuss the photographs.

Read as a work today, the portrait extends across waiting, meeting again and speaking about an image. The relationship between photographer and subject continues beyond the shutter’s release.

## Eight and a half acts.

### Odd acts: photographing

Acts one, three, five and seven centre on photography.

### Even acts: viewing and expression

Acts two, four, six and eight return to the images through viewing and expression.

The 2020 account connects this sequence with the seasons. The final half-act remains open in the public description.

## Participants decide whether their photographs become public.

This choice is part of the original project account. It allows participation without requiring a person’s image to circulate before strangers. This page uses venue and working photographs already published by P4.

Set beside Shanshui, Shixiang shifts attention from the assembly within an image to encounters between people. Set beside Human Surrender, it offers a route into the relationships, waiting and expression that form during filming.

CLOSE READING / 2026 CURATORIAL INTERPRETATION

## Shixiang

After the portrait is made, what position remains for its subject?

### Begin with what happens in the work

Odd-numbered parts centred on photography, even-numbered parts on viewing and expression; this was the published structure.

The 2020 project description gives participants a decision about publication.

An ordinary portrait session often ends when the photograph is delivered. Shixiang instead makes the previous photograph the starting point for another meeting. A person faces a preserved self again, recognizing it or finding it strange. A fixed image and an ongoing life occupy the same structure. Meeting again neither updates the earlier photograph nor guarantees acceptance of its interpretation. Photography extends from taking an image into a relation that requires further response.

A crucial formal decision is that participation need not cost the immediate public disclosure of a portrait. Decisions about whether and when to publish enter the project’s agreement, preserving the possibility of remaining unseen by present viewers. A work may therefore have happened without proving itself through an accessible face. That matters for this portfolio: richness is not the same as opening everything. Presenting structure, space and the terms of visibility can be more faithful than filling a page with private portraits.

### The limits of this reading

Eight-and-a-half parts, one thousand participants and a long-term museum were announced plans, not completed totals. This is not a current open call.

Based on the 2020 published texts What Is Shixiang?, Plant Your Seed at P4 Theater and the project introduction.
