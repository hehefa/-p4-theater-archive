# Last Rehearsal: ending after seven answers

Seven refusals and seven affirmations both end with “Start again.” Script variants, working credits and a white-cloth ending make closure a concrete question of making theatre.

## Seven questions, two answers, the same ending

On the final page of one script for The Last Rehearsal, B repeatedly asks A whether they would go again knowing the outcome, say the same words, make the same decisions, and face pain, love and betrayal again. A answers “I would not” seven times. Another script, also 28 pages long, gives seven affirmative answers: “I would.” Both end with C saying, “Start again.”[[S2]](https://b.p4theater.cn/en/research/last-rehearsal-seven-answers-and-a-white-cloth#ref-s2)[[S3]](https://b.p4theater.cn/en/research/last-rehearsal-seven-answers-and-a-white-cloth#ref-s3)

A small change reverses the answer without changing the final instruction. Repetition after refusal and repetition after agreement can place different pressures on a reader. That is an interpretation of the text. The files do not explain the reason for the change or identify which answer was eventually spoken on stage.

## More pages do not mean new characters

A separate 24-page script ends without these seven exchanges. Its second page already defines A as a woman, B as a man and C as a director, assigning each a different ordering of faith, doubt and betrayal. The 28-page script retains those definitions, adds a character-map heading and nested brackets, and moves the first act to the following page. A different presentation is visible; newly created characters or fully explained relationships are not established.[[S1]](https://b.p4theater.cn/en/research/last-rehearsal-seven-answers-and-a-white-cloth#ref-s1)[[S2]](https://b.p4theater.cn/en/research/last-rehearsal-seven-answers-and-a-white-cloth#ref-s2)

The extractable text of the first 27 pages is identical in the two 28-page PDFs. Their last-page text differs only in the seven negative answers; this does not establish that every graphic object or layout detail is identical. An editable DOC file also preserves seven negative answers and C's final line. Its filename shares a date with the 24-page PDF, but the textual difference prevents treating them as one edition on that basis.[[S2]](https://b.p4theater.cn/en/research/last-rehearsal-seven-answers-and-a-white-cloth#ref-s2)[[S3]](https://b.p4theater.cn/en/research/last-rehearsal-seven-answers-and-a-white-cloth#ref-s3)[[S4]](https://b.p4theater.cn/en/research/last-rehearsal-seven-answers-and-a-white-cloth#ref-s4)

## Starting again before the last page

Earlier passages give returning to a decision a concrete form. In the Disappearance passage, a male and female strand reaches an unresolved choice. The man proposes saving, and a priest confirms the save. In the Pregnancy passage, the woman asks to reload; after the priest does so, she makes a choice. Saving and loading are actions through which these fictional figures confront a decision.[[S2]](https://b.p4theater.cn/en/research/last-rehearsal-seven-answers-and-a-white-cloth#ref-s2)

“Start again” also precedes the ending: B says it on page 24 and C repeats it twice on page 27. Later, a minister narrates a king warming up, running and crossing a doorway, but the text does not specify what follows that crossing. The 24-page version ends with a metaphor involving choice and musical instruments; the two longer versions proceed to the seven questions. The writing repeatedly postpones closure without establishing either a successful moon landing or a death.[[S1]](https://b.p4theater.cn/en/research/last-rehearsal-seven-answers-and-a-white-cloth#ref-s1)[[S2]](https://b.p4theater.cn/en/research/last-rehearsal-seven-answers-and-a-white-cloth#ref-s2)[[S3]](https://b.p4theater.cn/en/research/last-rehearsal-seven-answers-and-a-white-cloth#ref-s3)

## Who speaks, and who moves behind the camera?

Another document, headed Script 3.0, makes working positions part of its image design. The camera adopts the director's first-person perspective; C asks to continue the fourth act and takes control of the lighting; the camera then returns to the photographer's perspective. On the next page, the director retreats behind the camera and photographer D takes the male-lead position.[[S5]](https://b.p4theater.cn/en/research/last-rehearsal-seven-answers-and-a-white-cloth#ref-s5)

This document assigns a passage beginning “You go first” and mentioning the moon to B. The 24-page and both 28-page Last Rehearsal scripts assign it to C. These differences show how voice, viewpoint and role functions are distributed across documents. They do not establish their chronological order or prove that real performers were recast.[[S1]](https://b.p4theater.cn/en/research/last-rehearsal-seven-answers-and-a-white-cloth#ref-s1)[[S2]](https://b.p4theater.cn/en/research/last-rehearsal-seven-answers-and-a-white-cloth#ref-s2)[[S5]](https://b.p4theater.cn/en/research/last-rehearsal-seven-answers-and-a-white-cloth#ref-s5)

## From a final line to a white cloth

A performance announcement published on 25 August 2023 schedules The Last Rehearsal for 19:30 the following day at the Ciyu Bookstore, Inside-Out Theatre branch. The announcement uses 最后的排练, while the script uses 最后的排演; this reading retains that distinction between the Chinese titles.[[S6]](https://b.p4theater.cn/en/research/last-rehearsal-seven-answers-and-a-white-cloth#ref-s6)

A post-performance article published on 2 September is introduced as Jiarui's account. Describing the ending, the writer recalls an agreed movement plan: cover the group with a white cloth, leave through the rear, and leave the cloth on stage. The account then describes a question at the exit which the writer identifies as outside the script. Ending becomes a problem of movement, concealment and departure. This is one participant's recollection.[[S7]](https://b.p4theater.cn/en/research/last-rehearsal-seven-answers-and-a-white-cloth#ref-s7)

A candidate recording whose camera metadata points to 26 August provides a different kind of evidence. Limited samples near its end show a white cloth being raised at about 1 hour 37 minutes, followed by frames in which it covers bodies. Later samples turn towards an equipment table, a bar and bookshelves. These separate images support concealment and changes of position; they do not reconstruct the obstructed action or determine whether the seven answers were affirmative or negative.[[S8]](https://b.p4theater.cn/en/research/last-rehearsal-seven-answers-and-a-white-cloth#ref-s8)

## Script identities and performance credits

The announcement places Huang Meng, Sun Jiarui and Xu Shuai beside the labels A, B and C, crediting all three as performers. Yun Jianhui appears under D as the documentarian. Zhao Xuan, Ru Meng and Zhao Ziyi are credited as musical performers, and He Fa as the behind-the-scenes producer.[[S6]](https://b.p4theater.cn/en/research/last-rehearsal-seven-answers-and-a-white-cloth#ref-s6)

Those credits establish particular work without collapsing script and performance. C's fictional definition as a director does not give Xu Shuai a real directing credit. Documentarian does not automatically mean both camera operator and performer. Nor does the cast list allocate every line of a particular performance.

## Keeping separate sessions distinguishable

Camera materials for another rehearsal point to 19 August. The later edit No Birds on the Space Station draws on that group of materials. It cannot stand in for the candidate 26 August recording or settle the seven answers. Searches of existing machine transcripts did not provide a reliable match; a missing search result does not establish that the words were never spoken.[[S9]](https://b.p4theater.cn/en/research/last-rehearsal-seven-answers-and-a-white-cloth#ref-s9)

This reading places textual states, published credits, retrospective narration and limited visual evidence alongside one another. The order and reasons for revision, the answers used in a particular session, and line-by-line performance allocation remain unresolved. Original drafts, private review records and camera files are not attached. No new listening check, transcription, visual identification or raw-to-edit timing map is claimed.
