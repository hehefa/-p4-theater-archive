# Seven answers and a white cloth

Scripts, recollection and a visual account in The Last Rehearsal

Read the two answers beside the same final instruction, then turn to a recollection and a visual summary of the cloth. The first two cards isolate the change made by 不 and the shared “Start again.” The third follows covering, leaving and a question in Jiarui’s recollection. The fourth asks what isolated visual samples establish. This is a reading order, not a chronology of the scripts or a reconstruction combining memory and candidate footage into one continuous event.

## 2 Seven affirmative answers

It shows what one character changes and what the final line retains.

Published editorial account of the questions, answers and final line on page 28

[Read: The 28-page text: seven affirmative answers](https://b.p4theater.cn/sources/public-last-rehearsal-seven-affirmative-answers)

**Documented details**

A second 28-page text gives seven answers of “I would,” followed by the same final line from C. The extractable text of the first 27 pages matches; on the final page, the extracted text differs only through 不 in the seven answers.

Participant recollection · Chinese

## 3 The cloth and the exit

It brings ending into bodily action, stage space and a participant’s memory.

Passages on the white cloth, the backstage exit and a question at the exit

[Read: Jiarui’s post-performance recollection ↗](https://p4theater.cn/articles/2023/2023-09-02-人类投降派-我消失了-在演完-最后的排练-之后-50a18864f8/)

**Documented details**

Jiarui recalls an agreed ending: cover the group with the cloth, move backstage and leave it on the stage. He also describes a question at the exit. Read this passage as one participant’s recollection.

Public editorial note · original not published here · Chinese

## 4 Lifting and covering

It offers a different kind of evidence from recollection while preserving the gaps between samples.

Account of limited sampling at approximately 1:36:00–1:42:19 in a candidate recording

[Read: Limited visual review of the white-cloth action](https://b.p4theater.cn/sources/public-last-rehearsal-white-cloth-visual-review)

**Documented details**

The public visual-review summary reports the cloth being lifted in a sample at about 1 hour 37 minutes in a candidate recording, with later located frames showing it covering bodies. Subsequent samples turn towards an equipment table, a bar and bookshelves.

## Look closely

- What does 不 change? Why does “Start again” follow both sets of answers?

- What is offered by the written instruction, and what by the recollection of covering, leaving and an unscripted question?

- Which actions can isolated visual samples establish, and which intervals must remain open?

The order of the two written variants and the answers used in performance remain unresolved. The scripts and candidate recording are not published here. Source cards point to public editorial notes or a participant’s recollection, not facsimile script pages, continuous footage or a checked transcript.

If the answer has changed, what still calls for starting again?
