## How the work took shape

Vortex is North Wind Theatre’s second production. In his signed article of 3 March 2022, He Fa describes a month of collective creation: some people left unexpectedly, others joined later, and rehearsals were both loose and tense. Each creator brought difficulties from their own circumstances. The notice names eleven members for the announced performance, including He Fa.

The text begins with a butterfly repeatedly beating its wings inside a screen, then moves through loops, lost signals, game language, love and uncertain paths. Rather than smoothing collective experience into a plot summary, it places differing voices in collision. Read with the roster and rehearsal account, it shows a work gathering from several people’s circumstances. The announced performance offers a temporary meeting, while the article retains voices that have not yet settled.
