# 3P: project-specific credits and participation

Thirty public studies. Anonymous method analyses and credited documents state their evidence separately. Interpretation does not establish implementation; this is not the full research collection.

English names and titles are editorial working translations. Publication dates, planned dates and actual event dates remain distinct; a published recollection is not independent verification.

## What the public material records

The Howlers article published on 31 December 2020 credits Zhang Shaohua, He Fa and Huang Lucang to 3P, with Qiu Yue and Guo Junda separately credited for photography. The Rose production article of 24 May 2021 lists Sun Xiaoxing, He Fa, Li Wenqi, Pian Ziwei and Zhang Haolin under 3P; Sun also receives a dramaturgy credit. It describes a temporary group following events through observation, participation, interviews and documentation. These are project-specific credits, not evidence of a permanent roster or reasons for changes. The overlap between participation and observation is an archival interpretation; authorship of individual records still requires separate evidence.

## Limits of this evidence

- Different lists do not establish departures or interpersonal disputes.

- An intention to follow the whole process does not establish delivery of every record.

- This compares two publications, not one merged event.

These are reviewed research texts. Related public films identify their access editions separately; local source timings are not converted into web links.
