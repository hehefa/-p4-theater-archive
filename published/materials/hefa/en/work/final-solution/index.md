## Giving the assembly the decision

Final Solution posits a fictional end-of-world assembly in which participants decide which roles may remain. The May test call allocated twelve deliberators and four candidates. Candidates made their case through non-violent means, and deliberators decided after questioning, discussion and voting. The theatrical “execution” meant elimination and departure. Rules for the June test, July questions and answers, and later discussions differ, documenting a model under revision.

In a signed article dated 14 July, He Fa recalls attending several assemblies and reflects on how the fictional situation prompted recurring questions about life and existence. He also describes a method of converting speech into text and preserving annotations and reflection. His 2022 retrospective identifies this work as a substantial event within Purples, co-initiated by Shen Bolun, He Fa and Shao Yifan.

## The work as a collaborative practice

The work moves theatre from predetermined dialogue towards procedures collectively enacted and contested. Candidates’ accounts, deliberators’ reasons, the limits of rules and subsequent disagreements become material for later review. Participants retain their own experiences and words; He Fa enters through co-initiation, participation and reflective documentation.

## Reading the versions

The project introduction and presentation proposal preserve different stages of the model. The fictional catastrophe’s deadline, roles and voting thresholds changed. Some documents specify a two-thirds majority among twelve seats; the presentation proposal specifies ten votes out of twelve. Each describes its own version of the game.

The Presentation Proposal places Coliseum alongside Duel Club and CCTV in a proposed continuous experience. The assemblies documented here and that later proposal retain their distinct status. The project trailer is a montage drawn from multiple scenes.

See the selected Project Introduction and source page 5 of the Presentation Proposal.
