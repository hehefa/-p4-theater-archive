# Archive work: preservation within the division of labour

Thirty public studies. Anonymous method analyses and credited documents state their evidence separately. Interpretation does not establish implementation; this is not the full research collection.

English names and titles are editorial working translations. Publication dates, planned dates and actual event dates remain distinct; a published recollection is not independent verification.

## What the public material records

A retained one-page work map assigns video to Huang Lucang, still images to Qiu Yue, texts to zoey, publicity to Zhang Shaohua and design to He Fa. The audiovisual and textual tasks include organization, classification and storage; publicity also brings project materials together. The editorial interpretation is that archival work is already part of production’s interfaces, rather than beginning only after a performance. With no date, adoption record or delivery receipt, these are documented assignments, not an organization chart for every period or grounds for blaming anyone for missing records. The labels zoey and Qiu Yue remain separate.

## Time and version

Editorial research from the cited published or retained documents; interpretations are not quotations from participants.
