## Research narrative: evidence limits below

The retained research notes connect reading a script with delivering lines in rehearsal. They open a question: how might changes in pacing, emphasis and the relation to action alter the felt experience of the same words? This is an observational question about moving from text to rehearsal, not a reconstructed teaching sequence. The notes do not provide a verifiable chain of demonstrations or establish who originated a delivery. The question remains open; a retrospective account does not establish a shared rehearsal outcome.
