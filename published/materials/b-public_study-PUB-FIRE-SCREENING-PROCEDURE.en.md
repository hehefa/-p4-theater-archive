# Fire Screening: objects, writing and conversation as a procedure

Thirty public studies. Anonymous method analyses and credited documents state their evidence separately. Interpretation does not establish implementation; this is not the full research collection.

English names and titles are editorial working translations. Publication dates, planned dates and actual event dates remain distinct; a published recollection is not independent verification.

## What the public material records

The winter-special proposal published on 10 December 2020 organizes screening into six actions: bringing an object, telling its story, talking together, jointly working on the object, writing feelings and placing that paper in the stove, and continuing the conversation. Compared with the retained short draft, the publication explicitly adds writing as a step, alongside timing and food-sharing arrangements. The editorial interpretation is that the medium extends from the screen toward objects and visible actions, giving expression a procedure others can join. This does not establish complete compliance, therapeutic benefit or implementation of a particular session; belonging to the same screening archive does not prove that an earlier discussion caused the change.
