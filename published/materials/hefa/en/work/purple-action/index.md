Further reading

Purples, also known as Purple Action, is a participatory art project co-initiated by Shen Bolun, He Fa and Shao Yifan. Its project introduction traces a precursor to Shen Bolun’s Coliseum in 2019 and describes its collaborative redevelopment in 2021. Participants enter a fictional world and generate the event through roles, deliberation and organisational action.

## One project, several theatre models

Purples develops different rules and live situations within a shared fictional world. The Project Introduction presents Final Solution as an organisation within that world and names its deliberative mechanism the “Final Solution assembly model.” Coliseum is a related development strand whose scope depends on the particular document version.

The later Presentation Proposal lists three presentation models: Coliseum, Duel Club and the Surveillance System (CCTV). It arranges them as a proposed consecutive three-day experience. The models, schedule and spatial configuration in that document remain a proposal, rather than proof that every component was implemented.

The Final Solution page brings together assemblies, rules and reflective writing. The Duel Club page preserves its calls, event texts and images. Fictional world-building, records of particular events and later proposals retain their separate status.

## From the project to models, rules and live records

Purples is an interactive theatre and multiplayer role-playing project. A fictional world, organisations, meetings and voting procedures structure live participation; actions and conversations in turn produce moving images, paper records, writing and graphic materials.

The project document identifies Shen Bolun’s 2019 Colosseum as its predecessor and describes a 2021 reworking by the Purples group. The predecessor, proposal versions and individual events are recorded separately.

The project document names Shen Bolun, He Fa and Shao Yifan. This project credit does not establish the photographer or designer of each individual image.
