## Drama and games at P4 Theater

Theatre Murder Case appears in the team’s project list in a 2016 proposal jointly signed by He Fa and Zhang Aoshuang. The source states that it was created at P4 Theater as “an immersive theatrical experience combining drama and games.”

## The collaborative archive

The same page lists Hamlet’s Mirror, The Invisible Odyssey and the Sangou GIF Theatre as FakingAosome projects. Although the surviving description of Theatre Murder Case is brief, it identifies a place of creation, a collaborating group and an approach combining theatre with games.

## Scope of the surviving material

The verifiable record is the project listing in the 2016 proposal. The page does not provide a premiere date, script, duration or full production credits. This entry preserves the documented project as a point of access for comparison with further performance materials.
