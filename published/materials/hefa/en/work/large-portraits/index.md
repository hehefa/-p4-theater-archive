## From the portrait to its details

The 17-page portfolio unfolds in four groups: He Fa, Yang Chen, Huazai and Bowen. Each begins with a full portrait followed by three pages of enlarged details—forehead, hair, eye, nose, lips, ear or hand. The sitters retain their own postures, clothing and lighting; they are not standardized into an identity-photograph format.

The sequence moves from a person to details, then returns to another person. A full portrait allows a face to be apprehended as a whole, while detail pages attend to skin, hair, reflected light and incidental gestures. Each portrait has its own recorded physical dimensions; all are identified as archival pigment prints from 2010. Names follow the captions in the portfolio.

## Another record of the same portrait

Portrait — He Fa in the 2010 portfolio and I in the 2012 record use the same portrait. The earlier image is vertical and has no grid; the later image is square and gridded. The portfolios give different titles, dates and dimensions. This site retains both documentary versions and connects them through related works.
