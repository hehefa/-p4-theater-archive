## A proposal concerned with loss of control

The 2016 proposal describes The Invisible Odyssey as a performance exhibition developed by He Fa and Zhang Aoshuang with the advanced study programme of the School of Intermedia Art at the China Academy of Art. Its stated aim is to address “loss of control” through actions and movements involving a thirty-person exhibiting group.

## The document speaks in the future tense

The source states that the project “will participate in the Shanghai Biennale in November 2016.” This records an intention at the time of writing. The entry therefore retains its proposal status, without converting the plan into a completed participation or identifying it automatically with another contemporaneous project.
