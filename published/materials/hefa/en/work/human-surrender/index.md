## Project statement

Human Surrender / Shoot Self with You took shape through casting, rehearsal and filming in 2023. Participants faced one another and the camera; initiating, performing, filming and directing intersected within a shared creation process.

At 19:30 on 26 August 2023, The Last Rehearsal took place at Ciyu Bookstore (Middle Theater branch). The final performance before filming wrapped combined theatre, performance art, live sound, installation, audience interaction and continuous photography; material from the event entered the film.

After completing the film, He Fa continued to work with its voices, dialogue, breath, images and excerpts through sound sessions, explanatory shorts and frame-by-frame notes.

The film's festival journey has three stages: selection by Filmmaker Sessions Volume 8, Los Angeles Lift-Off Film Festival, the Summer edition of the Los Angeles Film and Documentary Awards, and TheaterFilmFest - FIKTIVA in 2024; a place in the Boden International Film Festival's 2nd Quarter semifinal the same year; and Best Feature Film at the Esoteric Film Festival in 2025.

## June 2023: an initial three-part film proposal

He Fa’s recruitment text of June 3 proposed three parts: long-take rehearsals in a black stage space; a camera following performers into everyday life; and a public performance recording their interaction with an audience. Three people were sought to play a director and two leads, with work planned for June–August. This proposal documents an early method rather than the final film’s structure, cast size or duration.

## From a borrowed camera to shifting production roles

The production account of August 18 names Yun Jianhui as an early initiator and cinematographer. Zhou Jinsong and Liu Jinqi recorded the process while contributing ideas, scripts and production work. He Fa describes roles changing during the work: actors could direct, camera operators could perform, and recorders also acted within events. These contemporary credits preserve individual contributions alongside the shifting structure of the collaboration.

## The pre-performance credits for The Last Rehearsal

The August 25 announcement scheduled the film’s concluding performance for 19:30 on August 26 at Ciyu Bookstore’s Inside-Out Theatre branch. It lists Huang Meng, Sun Jiarui and Xu Shuai as performers; Yun Jianhui as recorder; Zhao Xuan, Ru Meng and Zhao Ziyi as musicians; and He Fa as the behind-the-scenes producer. The plan combined theatre, performance art, live sound and installation, with camera documentation throughout. These are the roles stated in the pre-performance announcement.

CLOSE READING / 2026 CURATORIAL INTERPRETATION

## Human Surrender / Shoot Self with You

When shared experience becomes a film, who can still answer within it?

### Begin with what happens in the work

The 2023 Last Rehearsal announcement links the public event to the film from the outset.

The end credits assign direction and writing to the team collectively; He Fa’s published 2024 account likewise describes performers working as directors, camera operators and writers.

The film overlays the reciprocal looking of rehearsal with the gaze of later viewers. Cameras are present from the outset, and people know their actions may become future images. The camera is therefore not a transparent witness outside the event. The collective direction and writing credits extend this relation into authorship; He Fa’s directing, initiating, producing and editing work forms a concrete part of that practice.

Editing still selects sequence, distance and duration; collective credit does not make those decisions vanish. The question is how the film lets voices continue to encounter one another and where it fixes a relation into one version. A published 2026 reading of eyes, sound, cameras entering the frame and questions offers entrances for close viewing. These remain versioned interpretations to be tested against passages in the film. It cannot simply become testimony that theatre has exposed real people, and a director character cannot automatically be equated with He Fa outside the frame.

### The limits of this reading

The film, public performance, rehearsal records and 2026 research text remain distinct versions. Private experience is not inferred from characters or performances.

Based on the 2023 Last Rehearsal announcement, He Fa’s published 2024 account, Imaging with You (2026), and the end credits of the 4g version.
