WORK WORLD / OPEN FROM HERE

If the sun moves, does photography’s promise change too?

## The sun in a relief

Central began with an ancient relief encountered during a journey: the sun was carved into the middle of a range of mountains. He Fa brought that spatial relationship into photography, assembling fragments so that a sun ordinarily seen above the horizon could appear among the peaks.

## A reorganized landscape

The work continues the photographic assembly of this period while organizing its composition around a specific formal problem. It offers a landscape constructed from multiple viewpoints rather than a single panoramic exposure. The portfolio records an inkjet print measuring 350 × 200 cm.

## Looking closely at the sun’s position

The sun is small in the complete work; the portfolio preserves a separate view of that area. Orange discs sit among trees, mountain forms and overlapping photographs, making the artist’s idea of the center of the mountains visible as a compositional decision.

## Where a centre is placed and where it is seen

The title Central specifies a place to look for, while dense photographic fragments repeatedly divert attention from it. A designated compositional centre need not coincide with what first catches the eye. Moving the sun into the mountain is the artist’s decision to rearrange pictorial relationships; the looking required to find it means that this decision is not immediately apparent.

CLOSE READING / 2026 CURATORIAL INTERPRETATION

## Central

If the sun moves, does photography’s promise change too?

### Begin with what happens in the work

The portfolio records a 2013 inkjet print, 350 × 200 cm.

The artist describes bringing the position of a sun seen in a relief into a photographic montage.

The sun is small, but the decision is large. Placed among the mountains, it becomes a new compositional centre. Photographs with separate origins serve a position that no single exposure witnessed. The work does more than manufacture a digital spectacle. It makes the differing spatial rules of photography and relief apparent: one luminous circle passes between media and changes how an image asks to be believed.

Central also challenges any simple story of relinquished control. It contains an unmistakable authorial decision: this element will go here. A landscape emerges not only from the contingent accumulation of fragments but from insistence on one placement. Pairing the work with Cocktail is more revealing than calling both photographic collage. One delegates position to a compositional idea, the other to file size. Different ways of exercising control coexist in the same year.

### The limits of this reading

The artist’s account of the sun’s position is a creative proposition, not a law of astronomy or photographic optics.

Based on He Fa Portfolio 3, p. 6, and the published 2020 text What Is Shixiang?
