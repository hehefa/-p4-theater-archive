# How to read the P4 event archive

Thirty public studies. Anonymous method analyses and credited documents state their evidence separately. Interpretation does not establish implementation; this is not the full research collection.

English names and titles are editorial working translations. Publication dates, planned dates and actual event dates remain distinct; a published recollection is not independent verification.

## What the public material records

The event archive separates public plans, retrospective accounts and records of actual presentation. A work may recur, while one recording may contain several phases. Roles are attributed only when a source identifies them. File dates, work dates and event dates remain distinct, and gaps are left open rather than filled with promotional claims.

## Time and version

Public archive method, version 1.0.

Evidence: editorial method, not a new historical claim.

## People and organizational roles

This entry adds no attribution of personal or organizational roles.

## Limits of this evidence

These are reviewed research texts. Related public films identify their access editions separately; local source timings are not converted into web links.

## People, groups, documents and plans

Solid: documented. Dashed: inference awaiting verification. Double: conflicting evidence. These relationships record public credits, attributed recollections, retained research and explicit links between research entries; no unverified identities or conflict allegations are added. Connections do not imply causation or implementation.
