## Participation in the institutional record

The Second Intermedia Art Festival brought installations and narrative spaces together as a large collaborative project. The school’s official page lists He Fa within the artist roster of the advanced-study cohort. This page records that collective participation. The curator and general director are separately credited; available evidence does not assign a specific installation to He Fa alone.
