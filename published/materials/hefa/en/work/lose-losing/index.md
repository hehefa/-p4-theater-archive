## From photographs to a projectable image

This 2014 work appears alongside the Wu images in the same portfolio, but its medium is specified as “any material suitable for projection,” with dimensions determined by the exhibition. That specification allows the image to leave a fixed print format and enter another surface and scale.

## An even field of attention

Pale grey, orange and cyan particles lie densely together, with diffuse edges and no single centre. Looking can shift from finding an object to sensing density. Compared with the more distinct clusters in Touch touching on the following portfolio page, attention here moves more evenly across the surface. This page presents a portfolio still; the source page specifies neither a separate running time nor a particular installation.

Archive reading, 30 September 2026 · Sources: He Fa Portfolio (PDF), p. 20.
