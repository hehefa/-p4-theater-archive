　　     中国画中蕴涵着中国精神，也蕴涵着中国特殊的
      观察方法——散点透视。画家观察点不是固定在一个
     地方，而是根据需要，移动立足点进行观察，凡各个
     不同立足点上所看到的东西。都可组织进自己的画面
     中来，表现出“咫尺千里”的辽阔境界。

Chinese painting contains the Chinese spirit, but also
contains a special method of observation of China--
Scatter perspective.Painter observation point is not
fixed in one place, but according to the need to move
the starting point for observation.
Where different footing see what can be organized
into their own paintings . Show the vast realm of
 "right close by thousands of miles,"


我开始思考，是否能用观察山水的方式去观察一个人呢？

I started thinking, if it canuse the way to observe the landscape
 to observe one person?


　　于是我使用微距镜头，以极近的距离去刻画人脸的细节。
并用不同的相机视角对人的五官进行观察。


So I use a macro lens, with a very close distance to characterize the
details of the face. And use a different camera perspective on human
facial features .

Interpretation of the work


　　按照需要从各个观察角度进行微距拍摄，详尽的展现出人本身的真实面目。
细节可能是令人惊叹的，可能是丑陋的，也可能是美妙的；只要我们敢于去真
正面对自己，放大自己，就会重新认识自我。

Observed from all angles as needed for macro shooting, detailed to show the
true face of their own . Details may be impressive, may be ugly, it could be
wonderful; as long as we dare to really facing of their own, enlarge their own,
they will re-understanding of themselves.
