## The work and its production context

A P4 notice dated 8 August 2020 invited audiences to Hallucination on 15 August. The brief notice preserves the title, announced performance and original visual. He Fa’s 2022 retrospective situates the work within a longer sequence: after returning to China, he resumed Shixiang with Zhang Shaohua, then worked on Hallucination; Huang Lucang joined the production, and the 3P group formed in this period.

Within this personal archive, Hallucination documents He Fa’s involvement in bringing a production into being. A proscenium play provided a defined production format, while rehearsal, collaboration and documentation extended the work beyond the stage. Reading the documents together helps explain the later intertwining of watching, performing and recording within P4. This account of continuity is an editorial reading across the dated sources.

## He Fa’s retrospective · 2022

“We then resumed work on the proscenium play Hallucination. At this point Huang Lucang also joined the production, and the 3P group was formed.” This retrospective is the direct basis for identifying He Fa’s production involvement and collaborators.

## From Shixiang research to an open call

In A Future That Seems to Be but Is Not, dated April 27, 2021, He Fa recalls that he and Zhang Shaohua had been developing Shixiang before seeking wider participation through Hallucination. He describes an online call, numerous applications, several interview rounds, cast selection, intensive rehearsal, and set and lighting work. His account explicitly says the production was successfully staged, adding an organizer’s retrospective confirmation to the earlier recruitment material.

The same essay places Hallucination, Cocoon and The Rooftop Howler in a sequence of projects assembled through open calls. Calling himself one of the venue’s organizers, He Fa also acknowledges that his judgments could affect scripts and collective decisions. The account documents production conditions and the organizer’s position; it does not supply a director’s credit, complete plot or final performance version.
