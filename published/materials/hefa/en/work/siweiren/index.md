## Images and presentation

The portfolio presents Four-Dimensional People as six square images. People appear in streets, beside trees or indoors, walking, reading, working at a desk or handling a camera. Bodies and surrounding objects are divided into rectangular photographs. Heads, torsos, hands and legs do not necessarily share a continuous edge; the backgrounds also unfold across blocks and fragments.

An individual figure remains recognizable, yet the pieces are not joined into a seamless portrait. The images retain traces of action, details of objects and the boundaries of separate frames at once. The title page records a light-box work measuring 512 × 512 mm, dated December 2010. It does not name the people in the six images, and no names are assigned here.
