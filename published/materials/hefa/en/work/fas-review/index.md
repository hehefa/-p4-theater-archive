## Four recordings

Fa He Work Sample List, preserved with material from the CCA period, lists four recordings titled Fa’s review: 01 runs 23 minutes 35 seconds; 02, 40 seconds; 03, 17 minutes 12 seconds. A fourth is labelled “dong dong perspective” and runs 1 hour and 33 seconds. All four share the completion date 28 April 2017.

The list also names advancement-review information, a statement prepared for the review and a statement for Fa’s review as important material within the video. This page retains the original English title, viewpoint label, durations and four historical links. The three studio recordings newly published in 2026 form a separate group; the two lists are not treated as item-for-item equivalents or as a complete event timeline.

## Newly published studio records

In 2026, He Fa revisited this 2017 project under the title Fa re-review. Three recordings are now public on the P4 Theater Archive channel: IMG_6298 provides a long studio view, MAH01308 records the opening discussion, and MAH01310 records waiting and the final discussion. Each retains its own shot sequence, camera position and location sound. They are gathered in a dedicated project playlist.

The artist confirms that the participant’s VR headset showed the phone camera’s live view of the same studio and review scene. A person in the room could also encounter that room through the device, bringing the review’s own viewing relations into the project. The account of the headset’s image source and mechanism follows the artist’s explanation.

## The artist’s statement before the review

An English statement retained for the advancement review repeatedly asks whether the speaker is an artist. Beginning with art education and artistic identity, He Fa connects artificial intelligence’s capacity to learn tools with human choices made when certainty and expression fail. It provides a contemporaneous textual context for the experiments with language, performance and identity in Fa’s review.

The statement’s claims about artificial intelligence belong to the artist’s thinking at the time. Their place in the work is preserved here without presenting them as technically verified predictions.
