WORK WORLD / OPEN FROM HERE

What landscape appears when file size sets the order?

## The “weight” of a photograph

Cocktail orders photographs according to the size of their compressed JPEG files. Images made in the mountains become files, then gather through sorting. The artist describes this as “settling or floating”: digital files appear to acquire material weight, and an arrangement of sky and ground emerges from their order.

## Three images

The portfolio records three works titled Cocktail, all dated 2013. They share a procedure while forming different distributions of colour and image. They are presented here in portfolio order with their respective dimensions; no new numbers are added to the original titles.

## Ordering files, reading a landscape

The three portfolio pages retain different degrees of separation between sky and ground: page 8 has a clearer blue-grey upper and green lower field; green and dark areas spread more widely on page 9; on page 10, a finer grid shifts from blue-black to brown-green. Small photographs remain legible as thumbnail views while forming bands of colour at a larger scale. Sorting supplies an arrangement; reading it as landscape also depends on habits of recognizing sky and land.

CLOSE READING / 2026 CURATORIAL INTERPRETATION

## Cocktail

What landscape appears when file size sets the order?

### Begin with what happens in the work

Photographs are arranged according to their file sizes after JPEG compression.

The portfolio records three works under the same title, with different dimensions.

Cocktail brings a usually hidden technical property forward. A photograph depicts something and occupies a number of bytes. That value depends on image information and compression conditions; it is not the actual weight of a natural scene. Once assigned an ordering task, however, it determines which photographs become neighbours. A machine-readable difference becomes a visual difference that viewers can read as sky, ground, settling or floating.

The procedure hands part of composition to a rule without removing the author. The selected photographs, compression settings and mapping from order to surface can all affect the outcome. The surviving statement does not specify those parameters, and a presentation should preserve that gap. The work opens a question about how judgment is delegated: instead of asking which photograph best resembles a landscape, one invents a condition and then looks at what it produces.

### The limits of this reading

The original algorithm has not been established as reproducible. “Virtual weight” is the artist’s metaphor, not physical mass.

Based on He Fa Portfolio 3, pp. 8–10.
