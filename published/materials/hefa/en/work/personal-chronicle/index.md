## How the project is structured

In Theatre and Fear, He Fa presents Personal Chronicle / PHA as a way to reorganise P4’s earlier photography, theatre, workshops and actions into a framework for continuing creation. It begins with a two-to-five-hour individual interview in which a narrator recounts their history and ambitions. The recording is retained, with a shorter edit proposed for circulation. After the archive is established, the protagonist can announce a project publicly and invite others to join.

The six starting points impose no fixed medium and may lead to rehearsal, screenings, writing, exhibitions or other actions. The rules propose one project at a time for each protagonist, a public presentation to mark its end, and filming and publication throughout. The transcripts of Zhang Lei and Yun Jianhui published in March 2023 place specific personal accounts beside the framework. This page preserves access to those records while retaining the narrators’ own authorship of their experiences.

## He Fa’s source text · 2023

“The act of filming must be embedded in the event’s coming into being.” In Personal Chronicle, interviews, calls for participation, practice and review are connected. The start and end of recording are also used to distinguish collective creation from daily life.

## Recording before the project: Yun Jianhui’s self-account

A jointly signed article of February 25, 2023 describes a specific recording session: in early February, He Fa set up a camera and recorded Yun Jianhui speaking for nearly four hours, then made an approximately half-hour edit for circulation. The article also introduces Yun’s documentary Refinding the Compass and announces a March 4 private screening with its director. The documentary belongs to Yun’s practice; He Fa’s recorded and edited material is Yun’s personal-history account.

## Yu Yu Yumu: from a personal chronicle to collective writing

The April 6 article calls Yu Yu Yumu the first person to initiate a project after the PHA call. He Fa’s introduction describes two recording sessions and links to a two-hour Personal Chronicle: Full Record. It then changes voice to Yumu’s own proposal for The Thirty-Three: eight collaborators would write fictional lives spanning more than two millennia. The recordings are documented past events; the proposed people and scale of collective writing remained the terms of an open call.

CLOSE READING / 2026 CURATORIAL INTERPRETATION

## Personal Chronicle / PHA

How can recording a person enable them to initiate something?

### Begin with what happens in the work

The framework starts with a two-to-five-hour individual interview; after creating a record, the protagonist can initiate a public project.

Programma, Program, Hero, History, Ambition and Ambiance provide six entries; public reporting marks a project’s end.

Personal Chronicle removes life-writing from the résumé’s retrospective format. A narrated past and a desired future share an interview. The record need not merely certify a finished history; it can supply material for a new invitation. Someone listened to by a camera can then become someone inviting others to act. The important shift is this exchange of positions, not an assumption that a long interview is inherently truer than a short video.

The long account, its short edit and a later project should retain their differences. A version edited for circulation shifts emphasis; a public report temporarily concludes an action without concluding the person. If this portfolio borrows the method to present He Fa, it must give history, ambition, completed work and unrealized proposals distinct temporal positions. It can help readers enter someone’s world without allowing the latest arrangement to replace the whole of that person’s life.

### The limits of this reading

Narrators’ own films, writing and practices are not reassigned to He Fa. Unproofread transcripts should retain their status labels.

Based on Theatre and Fear, published 22 March 2023, the public personal accounts and subsequent open calls.
