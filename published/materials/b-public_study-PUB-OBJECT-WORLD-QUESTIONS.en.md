## Research narrative: evidence limits below

After its discussion of Four Eyes, a retained research note records a separate proposal: small objects in a space would return participants’ questions from their own perspectives rather than provide answers. The notes then ask whether people or devices would respond. The research issue is how an experiential aim still needs a response mechanism, operating roles and a form of participation. The evidence remains at the proposal stage. This entry assigns no confirmed title, does not merge the proposal into the plot of Four Eyes and does not claim that the experience was implemented.
