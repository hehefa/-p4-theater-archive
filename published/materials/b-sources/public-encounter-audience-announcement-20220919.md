## 公开正文

预告允许现场自由穿梭、席地而坐，并安排演后十分钟合影与交流。它说明预期观看方式，未说明观众领取听审席台词或可在法庭段落随时接话。

The announcement offers freedom to move and sit on the floor, followed by ten minutes for photographs and conversation. It describes an intended viewing arrangement, not the allocation of gallery lines or permission to speak into the courtroom passage at any moment.
