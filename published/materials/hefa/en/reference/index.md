HE FA / FA HE / 何发

# Work records & citation

Titles, dates, media, credits and sources.

He Fa is the English name currently used by the artist 何发. Historical records also use Fa He for the same person. Based in Beijing, he works across photography, moving-image installations, theatre and film, and is a co-initiator and long-term host of P4 Theater.

Organised by each work’s place and significance within the practice, with completed works and research in progress identified. Developed early and co-authored works, alongside current projects with concrete research material, can appear in the selection. Early explorations, sustained collaborative practice and proposals have separate entrances.
