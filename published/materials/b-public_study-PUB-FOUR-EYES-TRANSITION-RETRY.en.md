## Research narrative: evidence limits below

The retained technical-run notes consider where chairs should stand, when to move them and when two groups enter or leave. One local sequence questions an abrupt appearance, proposes placing chairs after the light dims, and returns to the end of the previous passage to try the transition again. This can be studied as a cycle of proposing, questioning, choosing and retrying. A transition requires coordinated object movement under particular lighting conditions. The machine text has not been checked against the audio, so it cannot supply a precise lighting cue sheet or a complete cast-to-role map.
