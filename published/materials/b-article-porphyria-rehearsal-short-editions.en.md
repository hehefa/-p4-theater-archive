# Porphyria: how three rehearsal Shorts relate to their source

Enter the rehearsal through phone, scene-selection and hand-holding excerpts, distinguishing joined passages, single excerpts, published edits and unresolved interpretations.

Production records, full-file hashes and sampled picture comparisons; subtitles not listening-verified, current playback unverified · edition-study-1 · 2026-10-18

## Three short entrances into a rehearsal

Three previously published Porphyria Shorts can now be traced through production records to one rehearsal source: phone and movement, scene selection, and hand-holding with performed dialogue. Published on P4 Theater | He Fa, they run approximately 26, 31 and 29 seconds according to the publication receipt of 1 October 2026. They are edited excerpts with Chinese and English editorial subtitles, rather than full-length source access editions on P4 Theater Archive.[[S1]](https://b.p4theater.cn/en/research/porphyria-rehearsal-short-editions#ref-s1)[[S2]](https://b.p4theater.cn/en/research/porphyria-rehearsal-short-editions#ref-s2)[[S3]](https://b.p4theater.cn/en/research/porphyria-rehearsal-short-editions#ref-s3)

This note establishes where the edits came from, how they select material and what can be observed. Production records, full-file hashes of the source, intermediate edit and final upload files, and four sampled picture comparisons support the correspondence. Matching files does not verify every subtitle or make platform timestamps interchangeable with source time.

## The phone: two moments joined

The first Short joins two passages from the same source in their original order. The compared frames show people holding phones and interacting. Its title and editorial subtitles frame the passage around a practical question: the phone provides the lines while raising a difficulty about acting. This theme belongs to the existing edit and subtitles that have not been independently checked word by word against the audio. It is a viewing prompt, not a verified transcript.[[S1]](https://b.p4theater.cn/en/research/porphyria-rehearsal-short-editions#ref-s1)

Time has been omitted between the passages. The compact exchange in the Short does not establish the absence of pauses, other remarks or further attempts in the room. Nor does it show that the practical difficulty was finally resolved. The source note records the joined structure.

## Choosing a scene: the theme of a single excerpt

The second Short selects one continuous source passage. The sampled comparison shows two people looking down at phones. The title and editorial subtitles frame the passage as difficulty entering a scene and a discussion of which scene to try.[[S2]](https://b.p4theater.cn/en/research/porphyria-rehearsal-short-editions#ref-s2) This is the edit's interpretive angle. Without listening verification, this note does not treat the wording as a participant's confirmed psychological state or as evidence of acting ability.

Unlike the first Short, it does not join two separated source intervals within its selected passage. It nevertheless has a beginning and an end chosen by the editor. A single excerpt cannot replace the full discussion or reconstruct the causal sequence of the entire rehearsal.

## Holding hands: an observed action and performed language

The third Short also selects one continuous passage. A sampled comparison between source and final edit shows hand-holding, a phone being held and the participants facing one another.[[S3]](https://b.p4theater.cn/en/research/porphyria-rehearsal-short-editions#ref-s3) These provide specific actions to observe. Relationship language in the title and subtitles belongs to the performed scene; it is not evidence about the people’s private lives or actual relationship. Appearance, mouth movement and names in machine text are not used to identify anyone.

The three Shorts can be viewed together without turning them into a completed three-stage training programme or proof of a method's effectiveness. They preserve selected moments, not an assessment of rehearsal outcomes.

## One work, different dates and editions

The published premiere announcement supplies the work's name and proposed performance arrangements as project context.[[S4]](https://b.p4theater.cn/en/research/porphyria-rehearsal-short-editions#ref-s4) Its August 2022 performer credits do not identify the people in these rehearsal images or prove that a particular rehearsal choice reached the eventual performance.

The shared source is filed with rehearsal materials labelled “20220625”. Its timezone-bearing Apple creation field reads 2022-06-25 17:00:54 +08:00, while its generic container creation field converts from UTC to 2022-06-26 03:07:00 +08:00. The former agrees with the archive date; the fields differ by 10 hours, 6 minutes and 6 seconds. Their meanings and the device clock have not been independently verified. Both remain recorded without declaring either a confirmed event date.[[S1]](https://b.p4theater.cn/en/research/porphyria-rehearsal-short-editions#ref-s1)[[S2]](https://b.p4theater.cn/en/research/porphyria-rehearsal-short-editions#ref-s2)[[S3]](https://b.p4theater.cn/en/research/porphyria-rehearsal-short-editions#ref-s3)

## Publication status and further checks

The 1 October publication receipt and 5 October channel inventory record all three as public and processed. On 18 October 2026, YouTube's public metadata endpoint returned matching titles and the P4 Theater | He Fa channel for all three. The player pages required sign-in to confirm the requester was not a bot. Actual playback remains unverified: these are links to previously published editions, not a claim that a playback test passed.[[S1]](https://b.p4theater.cn/en/research/porphyria-rehearsal-short-editions#ref-s1)[[S2]](https://b.p4theater.cn/en/research/porphyria-rehearsal-short-editions#ref-s2)[[S3]](https://b.p4theater.cn/en/research/porphyria-rehearsal-short-editions#ref-s3)

This release adds no uploads, precise chapters or full-length source access links. Production edit points establish selection from the source, but have not been calibrated frame by frame against the platform, so no second-level jumps are generated. Exact spoken wording, other rehearsal passages, named on-site roles and subsequent outcomes require their own evidence. These Shorts also remain distinct from the library’s premiere trailer and other independent recordings.
