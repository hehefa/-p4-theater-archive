何发 《我的脸》

创作的灵感来源于在山中的行走。在爬山的过程中，我发现山的形状也因视点的不同
而逐渐变化。当我游遍满山，脑海中无数的景象映印在心里，就会真正感受到自然的
辽阔。于是，我开始将这种观察和感受山水的方式用于对人物的创作上。我使用微
距镜头，以极近的距离去刻画人脸的细节。然后用不同的相机视角对人的五官进行观
察，并依照我自己的解读方式将这些局部素材进行再次的融合，生成一张全新的照
片。 在这张照片中，细节可能是令人惊叹的，可能是丑陋的，也可能是美妙的。当我
不断地放大自己，认真的去面对自己的一刻，我开始重新认知自我。


He Fa - My Face

The inspiration for this project came from my experiences of walking in the moun-
tains. When I climb these mountains, I found that the shape of them could be
changed when I saw them in different directions. Because of these experiences, I
started to apply my observation and feelings about mountains into photographing
human beings. In this work, I used macro lens to get the details of the five sense or-
gans on the face and then observed each through different camera angles. With my
own understanding of these materials I grouped them into a new photo. The details
presented in this work could be amazing. While some may be ugly, some could be
beautiful. Furthermore, when I constantly enlarged my own face photo and seriously
looked at it, I started to re-see myself.


                                                                                         He Fa - My Face
