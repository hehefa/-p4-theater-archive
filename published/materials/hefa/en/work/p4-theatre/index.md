The idea of P4 began in 2015 and its first public work appeared in 2016. He Fa has since sustained its projects, spaces, moving images and writing.

## Project statement

Around 2015, He Fa and Zhang Shaohua initiated P4 Theater in Beijing. Zhang Aoshuang took part in its early conception and co-curated the first public performance, Shadow Counterpoint, with He Fa in 2016. He Fa has continued as P4’s long-term artistic lead and organiser.

P4 projects have entered former factories, a small office, a boxing space, bookstores and private rooms. Site, participant identity, audience routes and filming conditions are not backdrops; they directly alter how the work takes place.

## Enabling participants to initiate work

In his 2024 retrospective, He Fa describes 510 as an incubator operating from a living-room-sized space, where participants rehearsed and went on to form their own groups. The 2025 ten-year archive explicitly distinguishes projects initiated by participants. Together, these documents show how space, calls, publication and mutual support gave new work a starting point. This website preserves that shared relation while keeping the original creative credits.

## Meat Grinder · Kousi Theatre

The August 2022 call for Meat Grinder is credited to Zhao Xuan, followed by Kousi Theatre’s declaration and an announcement for a party-format performance on 1 November. Subsequent texts retain voices including Zhao Xuan and Miaoji. He Fa includes the project in P4’s lineage of participant-led work; this chapter documents that platform relationship without adding a directing credit for him.

## Kill Mother · Guo Qian

Guo Qian’s call sets out Kill Mother as a three-hour theatre salon for eight participants on 4 June 2023. Each was invited to bring a chosen text to explore relationships with mothers within a theatrical situation. The archive retains this specific public proposal; a separate 2026 after-show text sharing the title is not treated as proof that this 2023 session took place.

## The Old Sun Rises on a New Day · Jiarui

Jiarui announced The Old Sun Rises on a New Day on 12 June 2023 as a half-hour theatre salon planned for 17 June, with a poster credit to Wang Yiwen. The original title uses “rises.” Alongside the preceding projects, it remains part of P4’s participant-led archive, linked to its own announcement and retaining its creators rather than substituting the platform’s organisers.

## Present, photosynthesis, psychology, permanent

An early visual draft places four words beginning with P beside the P4 theater mark: present, photosynthesis, psychology and permanent. The P4 Preface layout pairs a photograph of the theatre with a text about mutually watching and performing: stepping beyond definitions imposed by others and holding a present moment in an image. These drafts preserve an early articulation of P4’s connection between photography, live presence and participants’ own experience.

## 2019–2021: shared photographic research and a public venue

A Future That Seems to Be but Is Not places P4’s beginnings in He Fa and Zhang Shaohua’s shared photographic work. Former photography classmates, they explored portraiture, performance and emotion, established a venue in Tiantongyuan in 2015, and continued after He Fa returned to China in 2019. He calls the space their laboratory and treats theatre as recruitment, rehearsal, disagreement and public performance together. The 2023 essay Salon of the Rejected and Alliance of Failures describes the 2019 shift as opening the black box to people with few opportunities to use a stage.

## Questioning the organizers within the interview

In the first Cocoon interview, He Fa is one of several respondents. He recalls telling participants that different roles did not remove their right to disagree, and hopes relationships might continue beyond the completion of a performance task. A Rooftop Howler interview preserves a counterpoint: as He Fa asks what a performer observed, Zhang Shaohua questions whether the interview imposes a prior assumption, while Huang Lucang notes its movement toward analysis. Together, the voices show organizing methods being discussed and challenged within the process itself.

## The public opening in a retained report

An opening-report manuscript retained by the artist records P4’s public opening in Beijing’s Diaosutai art district on 4 June 2016, together with the premiere of Shadow Counterpoint. This dates a specific event and is recorded separately from accounts of preparations in 2015. The manuscript describes Zhang Shaohua and He Fa as founders and Zhang Aoshuang as a co-founder, and records shared work on the theatre’s design, construction and technical systems. These terms follow the manuscript.

CLOSE READING / 2026 CURATORIAL INTERPRETATION

## P4 Theater

How can a container support works while letting them change it?

### Begin with what happens in the work

He Fa and Zhang Shaohua developed the early initiative, with Zhang Aoshuang proposing a theatre layout; a black stage adjoined white working and audience areas.

Later work entered offices, a boxing venue, bookshops and other spaces, developing particular forms of recruitment, rehearsal, screening and documentation.

Calling P4 a venue misses much of its work; calling it one enormous individual artwork by He Fa claims too much. It includes renting and adapting spaces, setting project cycles, recruiting strangers, forming collaborations and making works visible. Each task creates conditions and distributes limits. How audiences enter, whether a newcomer can initiate a project, and when rehearsal must end all affect the art that appears.

Its depth should emerge from specific arrangements and encounters rather than a method presumed correct in advance. The early pursuit of photographic truth, time-limited co-creation, participant-led projects and cinema’s later arrangement of the process do not fully coincide. A mature presentation lets these changes illuminate one another and lets individual authors remain findable beyond P4’s shared name. A container’s value lies not only in how much it holds, but in what it enables to develop its own direction.

### The limits of this reading

P4 production, He Fa’s organization and his individual authorship are distinct. Different venues do not create an identical spatial experience.

Based on What Is Shixiang? (2020), Past, Present, Future, 510 (2022), and the signed 2024 recollection, each read at its own historical date.
