自２００８年以来，我便开始尝试用摄影的方式来表达我心目中的中国山水。
在我的理解中，山水似乎呈现出一种徘徊、虚无的状态。事物始于混沌，却因果相连，
在明显且纯粹的意象之中隐藏着数千万已被解构的信息，信息在不断重组的过程中悄
然转换着他们所传递的意象，周而复始。在山水不断重复叠加中，或许柳暗花明的那
一刻即将到来。


Since 2008, I have been trying to use photography as a means to express
the Chinese landscape that's in my mind. It appears like a lingering emptiness.
While all things may begin as chaos, causes and effects are inter-related.
Amidst the obvious and pure imagery are hidden infinitesimal pieces of analysed
and then synthesized information. In their process of continuous transformation,
imperceptibly they continue to change the images they pass on. The process repeats
itself on and on. As the images in the landscape are incessantly superimposed one
upon another, perhaps the moment of enchantment will soon be in sight.
