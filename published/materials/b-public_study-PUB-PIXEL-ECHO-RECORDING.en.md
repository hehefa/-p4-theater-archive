# Pixel Echo XIII: an invitation to watch a recording event

Thirty public studies. Anonymous method analyses and credited documents state their evidence separately. Interpretation does not establish implementation; this is not the full research collection.

English names and titles are editorial working translations. Publication dates, planned dates and actual event dates remain distinct; a published recollection is not independent verification.

## What the public material records

The announcement of 31 August 2016 schedules the thirteenth Pixel Echo event at P4 for 2 September at 19:30. Its promotional PDF names Hong Qile, Mao Wengweng, Ren Yuan and Qian Honglin as artists and describes a performance-recording setting for Bwave’s internal works, asking spectators not to record. Attendance and permission to produce images are therefore distinct. The editorial question concerns how watching and recording coexist, beyond the programme itself. The notice does not explain every motive or establish a violation or dispute. Chinese spelling variants for Hong Qile and Qian Honglin remain documented; P4’s venue role does not make He Fa the author of the works or all recordings.
