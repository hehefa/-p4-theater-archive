## A parallel creative process

The eighth P4U project, Kill the Rose, was introduced as a “wild interactive musical”, with a story involving a leaked photograph and online violence. Alongside the production, He Fa authored a call for 3P creators to enter rehearsals, conversations, interviews and documentation. The public introduction of 24 May lists him in the 3P group and separately credits the production’s director, writer and team.

The 3P call welcomed photography, writing, sound, moving image and other media. It required at least three releases during the monthly cycle and proposed an exhibition in the non-stage areas opening on the day of the performance. Observers were considered part of the environment, capable of affecting the event. Documentation therefore had its own rhythm and public form, speaking while rehearsal was still underway and bringing participants’ judgements into the work.

## He Fa’s source text · 2021

“A sincere observer continually includes themselves within the system.” Watching, performing and recording become a way of working together. The exhibition and release frequency were proposed rules; this page distinguishes that rule text from the published roster of creators.

## Kill the Rose: 3P—Watching, Performing, Recording

What happens to an event when its record is published before it ends?

### Begin with what happens in the work

The 3P open call allowed multiple media and required at least three publications within a month.

The rules proposed an exhibition outside the stage area opening with the performance; stage-production and 3P credits are listed separately.

3P does not wait until the play is finished to certify that it happened. Observers enter rehearsal, interviews and conversations and publish during the same production cycle. A remark can already have been read, judged and brought back into the room before opening night. Documentation is not merely the event’s later shadow: it becomes a condition of its continuation. Photography, writing and performance cross one another through an actual rhythm of publication.

Acknowledging the observer’s presence does not erase differences between observer and observed. Editing power, publication timing and the consequences of circulation still require particular decisions. The original rules leave ownership for members to negotiate, showing that even the identity of a work’s author is produced through collaboration. A presentation should unfold stage production and documentation in parallel, with their separate makers and dates, so viewers can see one event generating different works that affect one another.

### The limits of this reading

Three publications and a simultaneous exhibition opening were requirements; each member’s actual output needs its own publication evidence.

Based on He Fa’s signed 3P open call of 13 May 2021 and the production / 3P roster of 24 May.
