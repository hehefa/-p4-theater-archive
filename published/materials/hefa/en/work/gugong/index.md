## Images and presentation

The Forbidden City appears in the portfolio as a single square image. A roof and the outline of a building occupy the centre, while colour, vertical lines and figure-like traces disperse around them. Instead of a sharply bounded architectural view, slightly displaced images remain visible together. Their overlaps darken; their edges become diffuse.

From a distance, the building coalesces into a broad form. Closer attention finds lines and areas of colour that cannot easily be assigned to one stable instant. The effect of superimposition is visible, but the source does not specify the number of exposures, the compositing procedure or an exact camera position. It records an archival pigment print, 520 × 520 mm, dated March 2009–March 2012.
