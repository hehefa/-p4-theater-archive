 Tell me, you have neither life nor death, in between？
 Tell me who is with you, introduce them, introduce your relationship.
 Tell me, who is the puppeteers? Do you like them? How is your relationship?

 04 As for the training of puppeteers, this process is complex, delicate and slowly infiltrated.
 It needs patience and understanding.
 The training of puppeteers begins with the physical training of puppeteers, and then from objects to puppets.
 The training of focus, breathing, sight and center of gravity in puppet performances.

 05 What do the audience see on the stage?
 T he key is that you should look at "me" rather than puppeteer.
 For example, who am I looking at and communicating with.
 Who does the puppeteer want to see?
 The puppet was noticed and became the focus. Inhale and follow the rhythm of breathing.
 The body follows th e rhythm of breathing to expand the action space.
 How breathing makes me act.
 Sliding, translation, unreasonable movement, etc. Walking in place, getting tired, the sun is getting bigger and bigger, and the wind is blowing.
 Start running, speed up, speed up, run crazy.
 Loss of a puppeteer, puppet reaction? Ask the audience for help.
 What happened? Recall? Countless possibilities.

 MENTOR INTRODUCTION

 ha Li

 Shasha Li

 Puppeteer
 Director
 Contemporary Puppetry Artist
 Founder of “Puppeteer Studio”

 Graduated from Shanghai Theater Academy; Twice won the "best performance award" of the national puppet art Golden Lion Award; Russian golden clown International Puppet Art Festival won the individual award "best performance award"; Shanghai outstanding artist of the year award; Won the Rockefeller Asian Cultural Association Scholarship to exchange as a puppet artist in New York; "Who's talking?" One of the artists; Jiangxi Nanchang Resident Planning artist; Resident creative artist of Longtang Miao village in Qiye Art Festival; Swiss cultural foundation 2022 resident artist in Switzerland.

 In January 2015, she studied calligraphy and painting in Shifang jingshe in Songshan until now.
 On April 1, 2019, Puppeteer Studio was founded. The Puppeteer Studio is committed to the research and promotion of traditional puppet art and culture and the exploration and creation of contemporary puppet art.
 In January 2022, she opened the column of "puppet art" in the bimonthly journal of "singing drama" of Gottingen University in Germany and served as the editor.
