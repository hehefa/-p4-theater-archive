## Building a theatre from a virtual character

A 2016 proposal jointly signed by He Fa and Zhang Aoshuang lists Sangou among FakingAosome’s works. It describes a “cyber-theatrical action”: using the internet to create a series of GIF images of a virtual character and collaging them into new theatrical and narrative spaces. The attribution comes from an explicit team work list, rather than assumptions about aliases or folder names.

In the public images, the simply drawn character repeats as a blue pattern, changes its posture in magenta lines and moves in loops against a green ground. A limited number of frames turns small gestures into fragments that can be watched again. Brief poems and wordplay add another voice to the figure. A body need not first stand on a physical stage: arrangement and timing themselves organise the encounter.

## Connecting two sets of documents

Five public articles from August 2022 place Sangou alongside solo dance, Baudelaire, Hamlet and Guy Debord. Four are credited to frankfaker; A Sangou Dancing Alone is credited to aosome. The 2016 proposal establishes the work’s relationship to the duo, while each 2022 publication retains its byline. Aliases are not forced into one-to-one real-name matches, nor are all later images retrospectively dated to 2016.
