## A publication emerging from a course

N/A is a San Francisco magazine project that grew from a California College of the Arts course. Chloe Ghillani’s public project page lists Fa He among the founding contributors, separately from the publisher and editor. This archive records that collaborative publication credit; the page does not provide a founding date or identify particular articles written by He Fa.
