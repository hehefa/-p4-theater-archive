## Research narrative: evidence limits below

A retained research note identifies a viewing structure: sampled frames show a carrying action, while unchecked machine notes offer several accounts around the passage. This raises a formal question: must an audience receive a single story as soon as it sees an action? This entry reads action and explanation separately, treating the accounts as juxtaposed narrative possibilities rather than choosing a definitive plot. The sampled frames support a local visible action, not its full duration, the attribution of every line or a scene-by-scene role map.
