# Hamlet Mirror: translation and two temporal structures

Thirty public studies. Anonymous method analyses and credited documents state their evidence separately. Interpretation does not establish implementation; this is not the full research collection.

English names and titles are editorial working translations. Publication dates, planned dates and actual event dates remain distinct; a published recollection is not independent verification.

## What the public material records

The retained announcement describes repeatedly translating existing text by machine and credits He Fa and Yang Fan as editors. This does not establish the use of present-day generative models. A thirty-minute programme appears alongside a later retained script whose prologue, six acts and ending extend to thirty-five minutes. The difference belongs to the documentary versions: it does not prove a five-minute performance overrun, and script timings are not synchronized subtitles or viewing-edition chapters. The later manuscript separates venue collaboration from photography. An editorial reading is that repeated transformation makes words unstable and asks spectators to reconsider their relation to bodies and projections; it does not reconstruct the installation process or the makers’ motives.
