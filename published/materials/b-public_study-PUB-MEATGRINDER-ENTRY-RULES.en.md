## What the public material records

The recruitment text published on 13 October 2022 specifies entry for actors, spectators and observers: personal relationships or preferences should not exclude spectators; strangers may become actors; recruitment or replacement remains open; and observers are asked to respond meaningfully. The editorial interpretation is that openness concerns both access and expectations of reciprocal action. Intense bodily rhetoric in the announcement is not a report of injury or evidence about participants’ personalities. A replacement rule is not a record of an actual cast change; assessment, changes and production roles require further evidence.
