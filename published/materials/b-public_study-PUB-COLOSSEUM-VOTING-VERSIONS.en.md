# Colosseum and Final Solution: changing rules for silent voting

Thirty public studies. Anonymous method analyses and credited documents state their evidence separately. Interpretation does not establish implementation; this is not the full research collection.

English names and titles are editorial working translations. Publication dates, planned dates and actual event dates remain distinct; a published recollection is not independent verification.

## What the public material records

Three public rules do not show a simple tightening of participation: the 14 April 2021 Colosseum recruitment permits voting without deliberating in principle; the 30 May Final Solution test notice disallows it; and the 15 July introduction permits it in principle again. The May article explicitly describes an adaptation of Colosseum, establishing a textual connection. The editorial interpretation distinguishes speaking from voting as separately configurable forms of participation. The publications do not identify a meeting or person responsible for the changes. The July rule is not necessarily a wholly new right, and fictional execution rules are not reports of actual harm.
