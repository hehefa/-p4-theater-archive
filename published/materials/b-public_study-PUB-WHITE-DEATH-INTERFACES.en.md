# White Death: distributing clues and responsive actions

Thirty public studies. Anonymous method analyses and credited documents state their evidence separately. Interpretation does not establish implementation; this is not the full research collection.

English names and titles are editorial working translations. Publication dates, planned dates and actual event dates remain distinct; a published recollection is not independent verification.

## What the public material records

The player manual uses search intervals and increasing action-point costs to prevent a few players from monopolizing investigation. It distinguishes obtaining a clue from being entitled to interpret it, making communication part of play. Character sheets distribute responsive actions: a doctor orders assistance, Carter is prompted to react with alarm and hesitation, and other fictional characters try to calm the situation or seek comfort. The editorial interpretation is that rules allocate opportunities while character sheets provide actions others can take up, allowing a limited stage to sustain a larger story world. Carter versions differ in undercover-personnel counts and action prompts; none establishes an actual cast, the adopted version or the outcome of play.
