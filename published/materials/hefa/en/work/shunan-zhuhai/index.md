## Images and presentation

The narrow vertical format of Shunan Bamboo Sea differs markedly from the square and horizontal works beside it in the portfolio. Trees, slopes, green vegetation and distant mountains are joined along its height, with white or pale grey gaps near the top and middle. The edges of individual photographs sometimes remain clear and sometimes overlap, making the image appear both continuous and accumulated.

The image invites attention along its length, moving between close details of foliage and ground and more distant mountain forms. No single central point contains all of it. The title page records a light box measuring 286 × 1016 mm, dated December 2010. Its vertical composition and proportions are preserved here, including both ends of the image.

## Archive note
