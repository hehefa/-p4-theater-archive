# Purple Action: when making a case becomes a proposal

How does dissatisfaction with speaking opportunities become an eight-plus-eight proposal? Proposals, replies, rule descriptions and trailer editions reveal relationships between expression, judgment and procedure.

## Without time to make a case, how can a decision be made?

In a forum-style article within Purple Action, a poster signed Yuan Jinkui, identifying themself as deliberator number 10, poses a concrete problem: if candidates cannot fully explain their vision of a world, on what basis can deliberators decide? The poster hoped for an encounter between different worldviews but describes fragmented questioning and unclear expression. This is the published position and experience of one textual voice.[[S1]](https://b.p4theater.cn/en/research/purple-action-speaking-time-and-rule-proposals#ref-s1)

The artwork constructs a world facing its end, placing participants within deliberation and voting procedures to decide who can become citizens of a new world. The texts explicitly identify the Final Solution organization and Haijiao Community forum as fictional settings within the work. Signatures below are preserved as documentary attributions, without mapping them to verified real identities. Survival, exclusion and death are states within the artwork, not reports of actual harm.[[S1]](https://b.p4theater.cn/en/research/purple-action-speaking-time-and-rule-proposals#ref-s1)[[S2]](https://b.p4theater.cn/en/research/purple-action-speaking-time-and-rule-proposals#ref-s2)

## From dissatisfaction to an eight-plus-eight proposal

The poster first suggests that everyone prepare a speech explaining their desired world and the role they would undertake. Five, ten and even twenty minutes are proposed for individual statements. The question shifts from what to discuss to how speaking opportunities should be distributed.[[S1]](https://b.p4theater.cn/en/research/purple-action-speaking-time-and-rule-proposals#ref-s1)

A reply signed Fuermasi questions whether a speech longer than ten minutes can be delivered well and calculates that sixteen people speaking for five minutes would require eighty minutes. Another, signed Haide de Menxia Gou, worries that long speeches would turn the encounter into a presentation session. Fuller expression may help people judge; making extended statements a threshold may also privilege people skilled in that form. The latter is this essay's interpretation, not a judgment about any real individual's ability.[[S1]](https://b.p4theater.cn/en/research/purple-action-speaking-time-and-rule-proposals#ref-s1)

The poster then proposes eight candidates and eight deliberators. Oversubscribed positions might be allocated by selection or voting; statements could be shortened to five minutes; someone would leave the candidate group each round and might join the deliberators or share a collective vote. The proposal imagines movement between positions as well as a different timetable. Its alternatives remain open; it is not an adopted charter.[[S1]](https://b.p4theater.cn/en/research/purple-action-speaking-time-and-rule-proposals#ref-s1)

## What the response promises, and what it does not adopt

A separate statement signed by the fictional Final Solution planning group explicitly answers the complaint. It thanks the poster, promises to investigate problems and readjust the parameters of the Kuijian Council, while resisting the advance restriction of discussion to a particular kind of theory or worldview. It emphasizes participants' ability to begin in their own ways.[[S2]](https://b.p4theater.cn/en/research/purple-action-speaking-time-and-rule-proposals#ref-s2)

The two texts place the disagreement at the boundary of procedure. The poster wants individual positions to be fully presented; the statement emphasizes freedom from a single standard for discussion content. It acknowledges organizational questions without explicitly adopting eight-plus-eight seating, five-minute statements or voting roles for excluded candidates. The poster also recalls an earlier session adding two-minute statements on the spot. That recollection is not evidence that the later published proposal was implemented.[[S1]](https://b.p4theater.cn/en/research/purple-action-speaking-time-and-rule-proposals#ref-s1)[[S2]](https://b.p4theater.cn/en/research/purple-action-speaking-time-and-rule-proposals#ref-s2)

## Reading the dispute beside two project descriptions

Both the 19-page and 24-page project descriptions connect meeting procedures, deliberation, voting models, archival work and dissemination. They design conditions for working together as well as roles within a story. The shorter document gives more space to members and collaboration; the 24-page document separately discusses rule design, stating that some institutions are built by the artistic group and others are opened to participants to construct.[[S3]](https://b.p4theater.cn/en/research/purple-action-speaking-time-and-rule-proposals#ref-s3)[[S4]](https://b.p4theater.cn/en/research/purple-action-speaking-time-and-rule-proposals#ref-s4)

The latter also expands the Saturday 6 p.m. Four-Hour Club: six fighters engage in explicitly nonviolent contests, members support them with votes, and the last remaining fighter becomes a member within a four-hour framework. Ballot examples also show rounds and abstention. These details invite further questions: who interprets abstention, who decides admission, and can participants change the procedure? They do not supply a complete record of an actual vote.[[S3]](https://b.p4theater.cn/en/research/purple-action-speaking-time-and-rule-proposals#ref-s3)[[S4]](https://b.p4theater.cn/en/research/purple-action-speaking-time-and-rule-proposals#ref-s4)

Differences between the documents make institutional and participant positions easier to distinguish. They establish neither a chronological sequence nor a revision caused by the forum dispute. Both archived article records give publication as 24 August 2021 at 21:01, UTC+08:00. July dates inside the forum text do not independently establish real posting times or the date of a session.[[S1]](https://b.p4theater.cn/en/research/purple-action-speaking-time-and-rule-proposals#ref-s1)[[S2]](https://b.p4theater.cn/en/research/purple-action-speaking-time-and-rule-proposals#ref-s2)

## An event also passes through different edits

The project descriptions connect recording, organizing, reworking, publishing and receiving feedback. Trailer versions make that production visible. The FA and v2 openings differ; several identical file-time positions show different shots; the version labelled as masked adds eye bars, dialogue subtitles absent from the sampled v2 frames, and different black images near the end. Its changes extend beyond masking the same images.[[S3]](https://b.p4theater.cn/en/research/purple-action-speaking-time-and-rule-proposals#ref-s3)[[S5]](https://b.p4theater.cn/en/research/purple-action-speaking-time-and-rule-proposals#ref-s5)

Two clips on the editing project's main track are disabled. Yet disabled does not mean the whole interval contains no image: an upper connected clip overlaps part of one interval, where samples from the FA export first show an image and then black. Samples near the other interval differ between FA and v2. Project references, final visibility and export versions therefore need separate examination. The evidence here consists of structure checks and limited frame sampling, without complete frame alignment or audio verification.[[S5]](https://b.p4theater.cn/en/research/purple-action-speaking-time-and-rule-proposals#ref-s5)

Actions and subtitles placed next to each other in a trailer cannot simply be narrated as one continuous exchange, nor can editorial order establish who responded to whom. Like the forum articles, a viewing edition is a produced point of access to an event.

## Creative credits and fictional institutions

The project descriptions attribute the initiation of the predecessor Colosseum in 2019 to Shen Bolun, and the 2021 recreation to the Purple Action artistic group. The 19-page edition profiles Shen Bolun, He Fa and Shao Yifan separately. Individual work and collective authorship both have a place here; the recreation cannot be attributed solely to He Fa.[[S3]](https://b.p4theater.cn/en/research/purple-action-speaking-time-and-rule-proposals#ref-s3)[[S4]](https://b.p4theater.cn/en/research/purple-action-speaking-time-and-rule-proposals#ref-s4)

The real artistic group and the fictional planning group or Kuijian Council occupy different levels. Member profiles do not allocate hosting, directing or editing duties for every session. Forum signatures, fictional offices and real creative credits cannot substitute for one another.

## What remains from the disagreement

The evidence establishes a textual sequence: dissatisfaction becomes a proposal; replies question its time demands and model of expression; another text answers in an organizational voice; both criticism and response enter the work's public archive. Whether an investigation took place, rules changed or a later session adopted the proposal requires further records.

An unpublished recruitment draft is not treated as the rules of this particular session. Machine transcripts are not assembled into a reconstruction, and fictional rituals are not described as harm to real people. No private originals, editing project or trailer files are newly attached. The existing public 24-page description and the two published articles remain accessible through the references below.
