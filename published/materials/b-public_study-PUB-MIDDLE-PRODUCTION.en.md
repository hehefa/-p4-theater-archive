# The Middle: coordinating lines, recruitment and a shared schedule

Thirty public studies. Anonymous method analyses and credited documents state their evidence separately. Interpretation does not establish implementation; this is not the full research collection.

English names and titles are editorial working translations. Publication dates, planned dates and actual event dates remain distinct; a published recollection is not independent verification.

## What the public material records

An incomplete retained planning note considers the volume of lines against a month of rehearsal, proposing two groups, a prompting device and backup performers while seeking a clear allocation of responsibility. Its late-April target becomes a 1 May announcement in the later recruitment and pre-performance publications; this documents a more specific plan, not the reason for the change. The May notice credits Chen Yu as director, Hu Junyuan as writer, Zhang Shaohua as producer and still photographer, and He Fa for event documentation and among the performers. The editorial interpretation is that a text entering a shared theatre must also become roles to recruit, a workable schedule and an allocation of responsibility. The sources do not establish adoption of prompting, alternate casts or replacements; photographs of paper-assisted rehearsal cannot settle those questions.
