## A thesis located in an account

The retained 2018 document Fa He.docx contains a brief statement: “Fa He’s thesis is a instagram account.” It then invites readers to search Instagram and supplies the link to chinesesamericandream. The artist identifies the account itself as the thesis, rather than using social media simply to promote a separate document.

## Document and platform

The document moves the entry to a research project from a single text toward an account, a rhythm of posting and acts of browsing. It establishes the account designated by the artist and the thesis format. Without a complete reading of the historical account, this page does not infer its post count, narrative sequence or present public status.

## The original 2018 record

The document metadata records creation on 10 May 2018 and modification on 12 May, with hefa as author. This site provides a readable transcription and retains the historical account link so the project can be read alongside Fafalish, YourTube and Fa’s review.
