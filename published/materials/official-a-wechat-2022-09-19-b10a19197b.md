 简介
 LIVE 展演“相逢”，是虫洞剧团 2022 年首次跨艺术领域联合展演。
 包括 戏剧、街舞艺术、肢体即兴、现场演奏 ，既是演员与观众的相逢，我与你的相逢，也是我与本我的相逢，是我们与时代的相逢。
 之所以 Live house 形式呈现，“相逢”既是一出充满惊喜的当代戏剧，也是艺术家通过音乐、肢体的镜像表达，像爱伦坡与乌鸦的对话，戏剧将亲密关系抽象成诗歌，引发一场关于生命的思索，你还是你，我也是你。这会是怎样一场“相逢”呢？
 演员切换在多个角色和场景当中，是医生、法官、空姐、恋人；是燃爆整场的街舞舞者 …… 他们也许荒诞的令人捧腹大笑，也许缄默引人陷入思考。该展演也打破了传统的三维空间，穿越到“虫洞”，引领观众游走在医院、法庭、航班、梦境之中。待临场者与台前幕后十余位艺术家共同感受、对话。“相逢”所要宣扬的一切，不必用语言，用你本来面目，这是编剧的态度。
 每一场 LIVE 在疫情时期变得尤其珍贵与奢侈，“相逢”在艺术空间限量人数沉浸展演，临场可自由穿梭、席地而坐。现场结束后留有 10 分钟接受观众合影、交流。如有访谈等邀约，请提前与我们联系。

 编剧/导演：李晶 街舞艺术家：POPOZOO 打击乐：杨晓涵 即兴嘉宾：王钧林
 Introduction
 The LIVE show "Meet" is Wormhole's first cross-arts joint performance in 2022.
 Including Music drama , performance art , hip-hop art , physical improvisation and live performance , it is not only the meeting of the actor and the audience, the meeting of me and you, but also the meeting of me and ID, and the meeting of us and The Times.
 The reason why "Meet" is presented in the form of Live House is not only a contemporary drama full of surprises, but also a mirror expression of the artist through music and body, like the dialogue between EDGAR ALLAN POE and the CROW. The drama abstracts the intimate relationship into poetry, triggering a thinking about life, you are still you, and I am also you. What kind of meeting will this be?
 The actor switches between roles and scenes: a doctor, a judge, a lover; A performance artist; It's the street dancers who rock the show... They may be ridiculous and hilarious, or silent and thoughtful. The show also breaks the traditional three-dimensional space and travels through a "wormhole", leading the audience through hospitals, courtrooms, flights and dreams. Those waiting for the scene shared feelings and conversations with more than ten artists behind the stage. "Meet" to promote everything, do not have to use language, with your true colors, this is the attitude of the screenwriter.
 Each LIVE has become especially precious and luxurious during the epidemic period. "Meeting" is a limited immersion performance in the art space, where you can freely shuttle and sit on the floor. After the end of the scene, there will be 10 minutes for audience to take photos and exchange. For interviews, please contact us in advance.
