## 公开正文

9月23日评论表达对结尾的感受，希望加入旁白，在旁白期间回到开场状态并让床铃响起。它给出具体观看反馈和修改愿望，不能据此确认建议被采纳或已有新版。

The 23 September comment responds to the ending and wishes for voice-over, a return to the opening arrangement during it, and the sound of a bed mobile. It records concrete audience feedback and a proposed change, without establishing adoption or a revised production.
