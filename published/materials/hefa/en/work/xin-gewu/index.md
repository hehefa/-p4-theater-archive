PHOTOGRAPHY & MAKING · HE FA

## A way of looking leaves a place of its own.

Light appears between folds; distance changes the form. Photography attends to these small relationships: moving close to a surface, leaving room for darkness, choosing a moment when light meets an object. Each decision holds something of He Fa’s way of seeing.

These photographs remain alongside his concepts, reflections on making and artistic direction from the Gewu period. XIN GEWU continues this thread of work, allowing the time already given to it to keep unfolding.
