## Research narrative: evidence limits below

Before one passage, the retained machine notes list a check of the starting point and music, reports of missing and then low sound, and a response allowing continuation. This candidate sequence makes technical collaboration concrete: what is heard becomes a condition for checking again and proceeding. Without audio verification, the notes are not a receipt certifying equipment repair or evidence that sound worked throughout the performance.
