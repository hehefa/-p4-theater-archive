# Forum theatre: making room for action on the second run

Thirty public studies. Anonymous method analyses and credited documents state their evidence separately. Interpretation does not establish implementation; this is not the full research collection.

English names and titles are editorial working translations. Publication dates, planned dates and actual event dates remain distinct; a published recollection is not independent verification.

## What the public material records

The 2021 Indigo announcements organize the activity into warm-up, a roughly fifteen-minute play and a second run in which spectators may stop the action and try a different response on stage. The 27 April notice credits Xiaohonglu as game facilitator and director of this event; the 30 April programme adds named plays and a facilitator credit. Their day-by-day schedules differ, and omission of a detailed 5 May programme is not proof of cancellation. As a method, the structure turns a spectator’s judgment into an action others must respond to. The notices establish an invitation and rules, not a verified instance of intervention, role substitution or resolution.
