# BODY: spatial proposals under production constraints

Thirty public studies. Anonymous method analyses and credited documents state their evidence separately. Interpretation does not establish implementation; this is not the full research collection.

English names and titles are editorial working translations. Publication dates, planned dates and actual event dates remain distinct; a published recollection is not independent verification.

## What the public material records

Cao Jiansong recalls abandoning a proposal to seat the audience around the acting area because of venue constraints. In the same interview series, stage and visual designer Gao Pengfei describes using simpler means within a limited budget, while seeking connections between space, speech, bodies, narrative and spectators. The two accounts identify constraints and responses but do not document a direct negotiation between the two makers. Gao’s broader interest in opening the black box and creating enterable spaces must not be treated as a list of features already realised in BODY. The final seating arrangement and performance version remain to be verified; a circular object would not by itself establish audience-in-the-round staging.

## Limits of this evidence

- The abandoned proposal is the director’s recollection; a final seating plan has not independently confirmed it.

- Two interviews placed together do not constitute a record of direct negotiation.

- No private budget or income is inferred.

- This and the rehearsal study concern different processes in one work, not additional performances.
